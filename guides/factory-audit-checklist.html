How to verify a Chinese factory: audit checklist

General guidance, not legal advice. For large orders, consider a professional third-party audit.

1. Paper checks (before any call)

  • Get the full Chinese company name and the unified social credit code (18 characters) from the business licence
  • Check it on the official National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn): status, registration date, registered capital, legal representative, address
  • Read the business scope: does it include production (生产/制造) or only trading/sales?
  • Check that the name on the licence matches the company on the quote, contract and bank account
  • Look for lawsuits or abnormal operation records (经营异常)

2. Factory or trading company?

  • Ask directly. Traders are fine for mixed small orders, but you should know
  • Does the address sit in an industrial area? Check it on a map
  • Ask for photos of machines with their own staff and products in progress
  • Ask which processes they do in-house and which are outsourced

3. Live video audit

  • Book a live video call; ask them to walk from the gate sign to the production line
  • See raw materials, production, QC station, packing area and warehouse
  • Ask them to show your type of product being made
  • Note the number of workers and machines you actually see

4. Documents and certificates

  • Ask for test reports for your exact item and your market (e.g. CE, FCC, REACH, CPSIA as relevant)
  • Check the report number with the issuing lab where possible
  • Management certificates (e.g. ISO 9001) are a plus, not proof of product quality
  • Export history: which countries, and can they share a reference?

5. Samples

  • Order paid samples from 2–3 suppliers and compare
  • Keep an approved "golden sample" and reference it in the purchase order

6. Contract and payment

  • Written PO with specs, packaging, lead time, inspection terms and penalties for delay
  • Deposit plus balance after inspection is common; avoid 100% upfront with new suppliers
  • Pay only to a company account matching the licence name; be wary of sudden bank-detail changes

7. Red flags

  • Price far below every other quote
  • Refuses video calls or factory visits
  • Asks for payment to a personal or offshore account
  • Certificates that can't be verified, or name mismatches across documents

An agent on the ground can do many of these checks in person. See also the Canton Fair & sourcing checklist.
