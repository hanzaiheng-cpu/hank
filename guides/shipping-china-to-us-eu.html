Shipping from China to the US & EU: methods, timelines and duties

Updated October 2026. General guidance, not customs advice; rates and rules change, so confirm with your forwarder or customs broker.

Shipping methods

  • Express courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS and similar): fastest door-to-door option, usually counted in days. Best for samples and small, light shipments. Most expensive per kg.
  • Air freight: airport-to-airport or door-to-door via a forwarder. Fast, cheaper per kg than express for larger shipments, but needs customs clearance arranged at destination.
  • Sea freight LCL (less than container load): you share a container. Slow (several weeks door-to-door) but economical for a few cubic metres.
  • Sea freight FCL (full container): the lowest cost per unit for large volumes; slowest overall once port handling is included.
  • Rail (China–Europe): a middle option between air and sea for some EU destinations.

Typical timelines (qualitative)

Express is fastest, then air freight, then rail (EU), then sea. Real timelines depend on route, season (Chinese New Year and peak season add delays), customs checks and inland delivery. Get a door-to-door estimate from your forwarder, not just port-to-port.

Chargeable weight

Air and express charge on the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight (commonly length × width × height in cm ÷ 5000 or 6000). Sea LCL is usually charged per cubic metre. Bulky, light goods often cost more than you expect, so measure cartons early.

Duties basics: United States

  • The $800 duty-free de minimis exemption was suspended for all countries from 29 August 2025. Low-value shipments are now subject to applicable duties, taxes and fees. Source: CBP fact sheet, Federal Register notice.
  • Duty depends on the HTS code and country of origin; goods from China may face additional tariffs on top of the normal rate. Check with a licensed customs broker.

Duties basics: European Union

  • The €150 customs-duty exemption for low-value consignments was removed from 1 July 2026. A temporary €3 customs duty per item (per tariff classification, not per piece) applies to eligible consignments up to €150, until 1 July 2028. Source: Regulation (EU) 2026/382, European Commission guidance.
  • A separate EU handling fee on distance-sale parcels is expected from 1 November 2026 (EU customs reform).
  • Larger commercial imports pay normal duty by CN code plus import VAT of the destination country.

Before you ship

  • Know your HS code and estimated duty
  • Agree the Incoterm (EXW, FOB, DDP…) in writing
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificates ready
  • Check restricted goods: batteries, liquids, powders, magnets
