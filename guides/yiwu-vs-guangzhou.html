Yiwu vs Guangzhou: where should you source?

General guidance from a Guangzhou-based agent. Every product is different, so ask before you book a trip.

Both cities are famous with overseas buyers, but they work differently. Yiwu (Zhejiang) is built around one giant small-commodities market. Guangzhou (Guangdong) is a trade hub with many specialist wholesale markets, the Canton Fair, and the Pearl River Delta factory belt around it.

Yiwu in short

  • Home of the Yiwu International Trade City, a very large market for small commodities
  • Strong for small, low-cost items: stationery, toys, festive and party goods, jewellery, small household items
  • Good for mixing many SKUs from many stalls in one container
  • Many booths are showrooms for factories or traders elsewhere in Zhejiang

Guangzhou in short

  • Many specialist markets (apparel, leather goods, beauty, lighting, hardware and more)
  • Hosts the China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair) twice a year
  • Close to factory clusters in Foshan, Dongguan, Shenzhen and Zhongshan, useful for OEM and custom work
  • Good air and sea links through Guangzhou and nearby Shenzhen ports

Side by side

  • Product mix: Yiwu = small commodities; Guangzhou = wider range including apparel, beauty, electronics accessories, furniture and OEM goods
  • Custom / OEM: easier around Guangzhou and the Pearl River Delta factory belt
  • Mixed small orders: Yiwu's single market makes this very convenient; Guangzhou can do it too through consolidation
  • Trade fairs: Guangzhou has the Canton Fair; Yiwu hosts its own commodity fairs

Which suits you?

  • Many cheap, mixed SKUs for a shop or online store → Yiwu often makes sense
  • Your own brand, custom packaging, apparel or higher-value goods → Guangzhou and its surrounding factories
  • Not sure → start with a supplier shortlist and compare quotes from both
