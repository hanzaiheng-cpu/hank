How foreigners can buy from 1688 through an agent

General guidance. Costs vary by product, supplier and route, so always get an itemised quote.

1688.com is Alibaba Group's domestic wholesale marketplace. Prices are often lower than export-focused platforms, but it is built for buyers inside China: Chinese language, Chinese payment methods, and sellers who usually ship only within China. That is why most overseas buyers use an agent.

Why use an agent

  • Talk to sellers in Chinese and negotiate price, MOQ and customisation
  • Pay in RMB through Chinese payment channels
  • Receive goods at a China address, check them, and repack for export
  • Combine goods from several sellers into one shipment

The steps

  1. Send your brief: 1688 links or photos, specs, quantity, target price, destination.
  2. Supplier check and quotes: the agent contacts sellers, confirms stock, MOQ, lead time and domestic shipping.
  3. Itemised quote: product cost plus every other cost listed separately.
  4. Payment and order: you pay the agent; the agent orders from the seller(s).
  5. Receive and check: goods arrive at the agent's address in China; photos or a check before export.
  6. Pack and ship: consolidated and handed to a courier or forwarder to your country.

Costs to expect

Ask for each of these as a separate line, so nothing is hidden:

  • Product price (and any price tiers by quantity)
  • Domestic shipping inside China to the agent
  • Agent service fee
  • Inspection, photos, repacking or labelling, if requested
  • International shipping (depends on weight, volume, route and speed)
  • Import duties, taxes and customs fees in your country
  • Payment or currency conversion fees

Risks and how to reduce them

  • Quality differs from photos: order samples first; ask for photos before export.
  • Branded or copied goods: avoid them; they can be seized by customs.
  • Restricted items: batteries, liquids, powders, food, cosmetics and medical items need extra checks or cannot ship by some routes.
  • Seller doesn't do custom work: many 1688 listings are stock items; logo and packaging changes need a factory.
  • Shipping surprises: get volumetric weight estimates before paying for shipping.
  • Agent trust: start with a small order and insist on itemised costs.
