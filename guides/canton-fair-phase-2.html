How overseas buyers actually source at Canton Fair Phase 2

Phase 2 runs October 23–27, 2026 at the Canton Fair Complex in Pazhou, Guangzhou. Check the official Canton Fair website for exhibitor categories and entry rules.

The fair is a great place to meet many suppliers in a few days. The mistakes usually happen after the fair: ordering too fast, without comparing or checking. Here is a simple four-step process.

1. Meet factories, but don't order on the spot

Walk the halls for your category, collect business cards and catalogs, and photograph products with the booth sign. Ask whether they are a factory or a trader, their MOQ and their main export markets. Prices at the booth are a starting point, not a final quote.

2. Compare quotes from 3+ suppliers and check 1688

After the fair, send the same spec to at least three suppliers so quotes are comparable. Check similar items on 1688 (China's domestic wholesale platform) for a rough reference on price. Ask each supplier about MOQ, lead time, payment terms and whether the price is EXW or FOB.

3. Verify the supplier and approve samples

Check the business licence, ask for a factory video call, and confirm whether you are dealing with a factory or a trading company. Order samples, compare them side by side, and approve one in writing before mass production.

4. QC before the final payment, then ship

Inspect finished, packed goods against the approved sample before you pay the balance. Then hand over to your freight forwarder with the right documents.
