What travels well

I source building materials from factories across China. Foshan tiles are one example; other major production regions nationwide are in scope too.

  • Strong fit: ceramic / porcelain tiles and slabs, sanitary ware, building hardware (locks, hinges, handles), aluminium profiles and window/door fittings, light steel framing and fasteners, decorative panels when packing is planned.
  • Ask first: paints/solvents and other chemicals, oversized glass, products that need destination certifications you do not have yet (e.g. some EU/US sanitary rules), anything that may need special export controls.

How it works

  1. You send specs, target qty, destination and any cert needs.
  2. I research factories / wholesalers across China (1688, production regions, Alibaba when useful) and return a shortlist with prices, MOQ and red flags.
  3. Samples ship to my Guangzhou address; I log, photograph/video and write a comparison against your brief.
  4. You pick a supplier; I help coordinate production updates and packing marks in Chinese.
  5. Goods consolidate in Guangzhou; I hand documents and cargo to your forwarder (EXW at the factory, or FOB at a major Chinese port — Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Ningbo, Shanghai or Qingdao). Full DDP is not offered — I can introduce a forwarder if you need one.

Honest limits

  • Written / async English only — no live interpreting or video calls as a default.
  • Export sourcing only. I do not take domestic construction or engineering projects inside China.
  • I do not run bonded warehouse or door-to-door DDP as a fixed product.
  • Destination certifications (CE, UPC, SASO, etc.) are your responsibility; I flag gaps early and can help collect factory paperwork.
  • High-level customs note only: normal exports need contract, invoice, packing list and declaration; wood packaging needs IPPC marks. Exact HS codes and inspections depend on the product — confirm with your broker before sailing.
