Dalian & Northeast China transport

Dalian is Northeast China's main foreign-trade gateway, with dense container routes to Japan and South Korea and services to Europe, the Americas and Southeast Asia. I move cargo by road between factories in Liaoning and the wider Northeast and the ports of Dalian and Yingkou, and by coastal shipping to and from Guangdong.

Who this is for

Japan & South Korea traders

Pain point: Frequent, smaller shipments on short sea routes; suppliers spread across Liaoning and the Northeast; tight vessel cut-offs.

How I help: Factory pickup across the Northeast, consolidation, and on-time delivery to Dalian or Yingkou terminals or your forwarder, with photos at pickup.

Machinery, equipment & auto parts

Pain point: Heavy or oversized pieces, parts from several plants, and coordination with vehicle and equipment export flows through Dalian.

How I help: Long and short-haul FTL for heavy cargo, LTL for parts, consolidation before export, and storage while you wait for the vessel.

Seafood & temperature-sensitive cargo

Pain point: Dalian is a major hub for imported frozen fish and seafood processing; cargo is time- and temperature-sensitive.

How I help: Coordination of suitable carriers between port, cold stores and processors; tell me your temperature needs so I can confirm before quoting.

Bulk and industrial goods moving north ↔ south

Pain point: Steel, grain and other industrial goods moving between the Northeast and South China face long road distances.

How I help: Combined road + coastal shipping between Northeast China and Guangdong, with delivery to the final factory or warehouse.

Overseas buyers sourcing in the Northeast

Pain point: Hard to verify suppliers and coordinate pickups far from the coast.

How I help: Supplier checks in Chinese, pickup check, storage and delivery to port, linked with my sourcing packages.

Services in the region

  • Delivery to Dalian and Yingkou ports or your forwarder's warehouse
  • Coastal shipping between Northeast China and Guangdong
