Guangdong transport: factory to Shenzhen, Guangzhou & Nansha ports

Guangdong is one of China's densest manufacturing regions, served by the Shenzhen and Guangzhou (including Nansha) ports. I collect from factories across the Pearl River Delta and beyond, consolidate, store and deliver to the port or your forwarder, and connect by coastal shipping to North and Northeast China.

Who this is for

Cross-border e-commerce sellers

Pain point: Many small suppliers, frequent replenishment, strict labelling and warehouse appointment rules at e-commerce consolidators around Nansha and Shenzhen.

How I help: Multi-supplier pickup, carton and label photo checks, consolidation and on-time delivery to your consolidator or forwarder.

Furniture buyers (Foshan and around)

Pain point: Bulky, fragile cargo; Guangdong is one of China's largest furniture-exporting provinces; mixed loads from several factories.

How I help: FTL/LTL pickup, consolidation into full containers, packing checks with photos, storage until the container is ready.

Lighting buyers (Zhongshan Guzhen)

Pain point: Guzhen is a global lighting cluster with thousands of small producers; orders often come from several workshops.

How I help: Collection from multiple workshops, quantity checks, consolidation and delivery to Zhongshan, Guangzhou or Shenzhen ports or your forwarder.

Electronics & small appliance buyers

Pain point: High-value goods from Shenzhen and Pearl River Delta suppliers; some items need extra handling (e.g. batteries).

How I help: Careful handling, photo records at pickup, and coordination with your forwarder on documents for sensitive goods.

Canton Fair buyers

Pain point: After the fair, orders are spread across many suppliers in different cities.

How I help: One partner for pickup, storage and consolidation, plus sourcing follow-up in Chinese.

Services in the region

  • Road transport across Guangdong and beyond: long and short haul, FTL and LTL
  • Delivery to Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Nansha ports, or your forwarder's warehouse
  • Coastal shipping between Guangdong and North/Northeast China
  • Cargo storage before consolidation or export: storage available nationwide, with inventory and photo checks
  • Pickup photo and quantity checks
