Visa-free transit: check if it applies to you

According to China's National Immigration Administration, the 240-hour visa-free transit policy currently covers nationals of 57 countries (including the US, UK, Canada, Australia and many European countries) who hold an ordinary passport and a confirmed onward ticket to a third country or region. They may stay up to 10 days in the permitted areas, and business activities are allowed. Rules, countries and ports change, so always check the official source before booking: National Immigration Administration: Visa-Free Transit Policies.
