FAQ

Quick answers before you reach out.

Do I need to message you before ordering?

Yes. Send the product and quantity first so I can confirm I can help and give a realistic timeline.

Can you buy and ship the goods for me?

Sourcing and quote comparison come first. Purchasing, QC and shipping coordination can be scoped separately after you pick a supplier.

Do you only work with Alibaba?

No. I also check 1688 and Guangzhou-area factories and wholesalers when that fits the product.

What if no good supplier turns up?

You still get my research notes and why options were rejected. We can adjust the brief for a revision.

Can you do quality inspection?

Basic photo and video checks on samples or before shipment, yes. Full third-party AQL reports are out of scope unless we hire a local inspector as a custom add-on.

Who pays for samples?

Sample and courier costs are paid by the buyer at cost, agreed before anything is ordered.
