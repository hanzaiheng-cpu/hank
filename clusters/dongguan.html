Sourcing contract manufacturing in Dongguan

Dongguan, between Guangzhou and Shenzhen, is known for factories that make products to the buyer's own design: plastics, metal parts, electronics assembly, shoes, bags, toys and packaging.

What Dongguan makes

  • OEM/ODM production to your drawings or samples
  • Plastic injection, metal stamping and CNC parts
  • Shoes, bags, garments and packaging

What I can do for you in Dongguan
