Industrial cluster · Foshan

Sourcing furniture, ceramics & aluminium in Foshan

Foshan, next to Guangzhou, is a major manufacturing base for furniture (Shunde and around), building ceramics and aluminium profiles. Orders here are often bulky and come from several factories.

What Foshan makes

  • Furniture: sofas, tables, chairs, cabinets, outdoor furniture
  • Ceramic tiles, sanitary ware and other building ceramics
  • Aluminium profiles, doors and windows

What I can do for you in Foshan

  • Supplier search and shortlisting in Chinese (factories, 1688 and local markets)
  • Factory visits with photos and video, or a live video call from the floor
  • Sample checks and pre-shipment inspection
  • Pickup and consolidation in Guangzhou, then delivery to the port or your forwarder

What to watch out for

Bulky, fragile cargo: check packing and carton marks before loading, and consolidate several factories into one container.
