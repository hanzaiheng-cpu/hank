Tiles and slabs per container

See how many boxes fit before the container runs out of payload or cube. Tile is dense, so the weight limit is usually reached first. That is why a 20GP is the common choice.

A 20GP is often rated around 26–28 t. For tiles, 25 t is a practical planning limit, so that is the starting payload. A 40HQ has much more cube (the starting figure is 76 m³) but a similar gross-weight ceiling, so the starting payload is 26 t. Replace both numbers with your forwarder's limits. If you type a box weight, that gross weight is used and density is ignored. Density is only a fallback, and ceramic body weight is often about 2.3–2.4 g/cm³.
