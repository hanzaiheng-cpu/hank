What the terms mean here

EXW — Ex Works

The seller's price at their door. You arrange and pay to move the goods, unless the contract says otherwise. In this estimator the EXW total is the unit price times quantity.

FOB — Free On Board

EXW plus the cost of getting the goods through export and onto the ship. Here that added cost is inland trucking to the port plus port and export clearance. Ocean freight and insurance are not in the FOB figure.

CIF — Cost, Insurance and Freight

FOB plus ocean freight and insurance to the destination port. Insurance is a percentage of the CIF value, so the CIF total is (FOB + ocean freight) divided by (1 − insurance rate). The default rate is 0.3% of that CIF value. Change it to the rate your insurer quoted.
